          Assumptions & notes

          Peak demand is the sum of count × bandwidth for all clients; multiplied by concurrency, it gives the effective load on a radio. That load is compared with the planning goodput of this radio — the throughput it actually carries, in practice roughly a third to half of its PHY data rate, and deliberately conservative on 2.4 GHz. It is not the data rate from the datasheet. Up to 60% utilization one AP is enough; above 85% it is overloaded. One AP carries all of its radios, so the recommendation is set by the radio with the highest requirement. All defaults are editable and deliberately conservative planning values.
