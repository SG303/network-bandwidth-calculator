        Assumptions & notes

        Peak demand is the sum of count × bandwidth for all clients; multiplied by concurrency, it gives the effective load on one AP radio. That load is compared with the planning capacity, about 40–45% of the PHY data rate. Up to 60% utilization one AP is enough; above 85% it is overloaded. The AP recommendation plans for at most 60% per AP. All defaults are editable and deliberately conservative planning values.
